Welcome to Refract

DLSS 5 for your games in four steps. Everything is reversible.

  1. 1
    Pick a game

    Refract finds Steam, Epic, GOG, Ubisoft, Xbox and EA installs. Use for anything else.

  2. 2
    Enable DLSS 5

    In the game's Setup tab. Refract installs ReShade, the RenoDX add-on and the neural-rendering runtime from inside the app — RTX 30, 40 and 50 cards. If a game can't take it (Vulkan, no DLSS), turn on Neural Screen there instead.

  3. 3
    Set up the game once

    Display mode Borderless, and turn DLSS or DLAA on in its graphics menu. DLSS 5 runs on top of the game's DLSS.

  4. 4
    Play and tune

    Press Home in game for ReShade → Add-ons → RenoDX. Ctrl + Alt + R opens Refract's overlay. Restore original undoes everything.

Don't use it in online or anti-cheat games — ReShade can get you banned there.
